Thermo-Mechanical Properties of Soft Candy: Application of Time-Temperature Superposition to Mimic Response at High Deformation Rates

Food Biophysics

The study examines the effects of temperature and time scale on thermo-mechanical properties of different soft candies. Time-temperature superposition was applied to small amplitude oscillatory shear experiments, resulting in master curves covering a frequency window up to 10^5 rad/s, relevant for rapid processing. The research highlights the influence of crystalline phases on material properties and provides insights into optimizing candy formulations for efficient processing.
